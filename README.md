# Biomimetic-Jumping-Robot-with-Soft-Actuation

## Project Aim
Designed and developed a **soft robotic system** capable of **vertical lift-off and controlled gliding**.  
Inspired by biological jump-glide strategies, the system combines a **spring-powered jumping mechanism** with **foldable wings**, paving the way for applications in **surveillance, terrain mapping, and exploration**.

---

## Motivation
Nature demonstrates efficient **jumping and gliding** in insects and small animals.  
This project seeks to replicate those strategies in robotics for:
- Rapid deployment in difficult terrains  
- Lightweight and energy-efficient aerial mobility  
- Expanding exploration beyond the limits of wheeled robots or traditional drones  

---

## Methodology

### Prototype Development
- Built multiple **physical prototypes** with variations in size, weight, and spring configuration  
- Used **CAD tools** for structural design and refinement  
- Tested mechanical stages: free stage, compression, trigger, and release  

### Jumping Mechanism
- **Spring-actuated** power storage and release system  
- **Trigger mechanism** to control lift-off sequence  
- Iterative testing to optimize **jump height** and **stability**  

### Wing Integration (Conceptual Phase)
- Designed **foldable wings** to auto-deploy during ascent  
- Gliding concept tested in **early prototypes** (full integration planned for future iterations)  

---

## Highlights
- ✅ Successfully achieved **10 ft vertical lift-off** with soft robotic prototype  
- ✅ Demonstrated feasibility of combining **jumping + gliding** in a lightweight platform  
- ✅ Validated **spring-actuated system** for consistent vertical propulsion  
- ✅ Established baseline for **future automatic wing deployment**  

---

## Limitations
- Limited jump height due to **spring energy constraints**  
- Structural optimization needed for **higher lift-off**  
- Stability issues during descent without **wing deployment**  

---

## Future Directions
- Increase jump height to **60–80 ft** via spring and structural optimization  
- Implement **automatic wing deployment** for gliding stability  
- Integrate **IMU sensors** for active flight stabilization  
- Add **lightweight cameras & mapping sensors** for exploration missions  
- Explore **swarm-based deployment** for large-area monitoring  

---

## Takeaway
This project demonstrates the feasibility of a **soft robotic jumping-glider platform**, achieving **vertical lift-off** and conceptual **wing deployment**.  
It lays the foundation for future **bio-inspired aerial mobility systems** that are **lightweight, adaptive, and scalable** for exploration missions.
